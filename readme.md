# Bayesian Statistics and Variational Inference

This simple preprint is meant to serve as an introduction to the topics of Bayesian Statistics and Variational Inference. It is aimed at final year Undergraduates in Physics / Mathematics who have thus far been forced to see the world through an expressly non-Bayesian lens. It covers some of the basics of Bayesian statistics and probability, before moving onto discussions of Variational Inference and Information Theory.
